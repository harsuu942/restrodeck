Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before switching to RestroDeck

What happens if my restaurant's WiFi/Internet disconnects?

RestroDeck features local browser storage caching. You can continue taking orders on tables, printing KOTs to local thermal printers, and settling bills. As soon as connectivity returns, all orders sync automatically to the cloud.

Do I need to buy new POS machines or printers?

No! RestroDeck works on your existing Windows laptops, desktop PCs, iPads, Android tablets, touch terminals, and smartphones. It also supports standard 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers (USB, LAN, and Bluetooth).

Can I import my existing menu from Petpooja?

Yes! Simply send us your Petpooja menu Excel file or PDF, and our team will convert and upload all dishes, categories, taxes, and prices into your RestroDeck account within 15 minutes.

How does customer money settle with UPI?

Unlike aggregators who hold your funds for 3 to 7 days, RestroDeck uses direct UPI QR codes tied to your own bank VPA. 100% of diner payments go immediately into your restaurant's bank account with 0% gateway commission.
